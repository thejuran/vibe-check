<interfaces>
The printed-path rule, as `90-finalize.md` states it for `BLOBFILE`: the block that sets a value prints it as `NAME=<value>` (`printf 'NAME=%s\n' "$NAME"`), and a later block uses the printed literal, with the sentence form "`"$NAME"` here is the literal printed after `NAME=` — substitute it; a shell variable does not survive into a separate Bash call."

Fix shapes, in order of preference (least growth first):
1. **Merge.** When no model decision or tool call sits between the setter block and the reader block, move the reader's lines into the setter block. Removes a block; adds no paste.
2. **Re-derive.** When the reader can compute the value itself in one line identical to the setter's (as `2c-codex-kickoff.md` re-binds `TIMEOUT_BIN`), repeat that line at the top of the reader block.
3. **Print and substitute.** The setter prints `NAME=<value>`; the reader's prose gets the substitution sentence.

Counting note (rule v1): a printed name ending in `FILE` makes every later `"$NAME"` use on the path a counted step; other names add bytes only.

`X` = the extraction-only commit named in `63-COUNTS.md` `## After the extraction alone (commit …)`. `BASELINE = dd8e4ab42fd0e8bbdaa445df7db2d58621ab18a4`.
</interfaces>
