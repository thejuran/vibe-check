<objective>
Make `worktree_snap.py collect` report which reviewed-side lines were removed since the last review, so the review can tell a deleted decided line from a line that only shifted (the evidence 66-07 found missing).

Purpose: this is the helper half of the owner-chosen wider repair for 999.44 shape 1 (SC3 / FRESH-03). Change ranges carry new-side line numbers only, so a deletion at line 10 and an insertion covering line 10 look the same. Plan 66-11 consumes this output in the step-0 prose; this plan changes no review behavior on its own.

Output: `removed_from_diff`, `since_both`, a third `collect` output line, a two-sided differential against the literal 2.15.0 helper, and mutation checks.
</objective>
