<objective>
Produce the data the D-04/D-05 card wording needs: which other spot (another copy of the line, or a finding grouped in the row) changed and caused the reopen. score.py records `nearby_spots` on the stamped snapshot (and carries it across quiet passes); carry_state derives the CR-02 copy spot at read time and exposes one reader, `stale_spots`, that batch_card (59-05) renders from.

Purpose: STALE-02 requires the card to state the CORRECT reopen reason; D-04/D-05 define it. Keeping production of the data (this plan) separate from rendering (59-05) keeps each plan within budget and lets the wording tests bind to a stable reader.
Output: `nearby_spots` in snapshots; `carry_state.stale_spots`; real-chain assertions for CR-01 (copy), CR-04 (grouped), CR-02 (derived copy), multi-spot (lowest line + count), quiet-pass carry; a mutant.
</objective>
