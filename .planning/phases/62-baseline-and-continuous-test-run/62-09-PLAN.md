|----------|-------------|

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-62-45 | Elevation of privilege | executor merging or deciding in the owner's place | mitigate | Blocking checkpoint; the merge and the go/no-go are recorded only from the owner's own answer; acceptance requires the merge not be made by the executor on its own |

| T-62-48 | Elevation of privilege | unapproved push to `main` | mitigate | The closing commit stays local; the push is returned to the main session for the owner's go-ahead |
